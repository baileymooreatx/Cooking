# Instant Pot Pork Chops

Cooking **pork chops with onions and mushrooms** in an **Instant Pot**
yields tender, fall-apart meat smothered in a rich, savory gravy in under
an hour. The key to success is **searing the chops first** to lock in
flavor and using a **cornstarch slurry or cream** at the end to thicken
the natural juices into a perfect sauce.

## Ingredients

* **Pork Chops:** 4 boneless or bone-in chops (about 1-inch thick).
* **Aromatics:** 1 medium onion (sliced or diced), 8–10 oz mushrooms (sliced),
  and 3–4 cloves garlic (minced).
* **Liquid:** 1 cup chicken or beef broth (essential for building pressure).
* **Fats:** 2–3 tablespoons oil or butter for searing and sautéing.
* **Seasonings:** Salt, black pepper, dried thyme, sage, or rosemary to taste.
* **Thickener (Optional):** 1 tablespoon cornstarch mixed with 2 tablespoons
  water, or ½ cup heavy cream/sour cream.

## Step-by-step instructions

1. **Sear the Pork:** Set the Instant Pot to **Sauté** mode. Add oil and once
   hot, season pork chops with salt and pepper. Sear for 3–4 minutes per side
   until golden brown. Remove chops and set aside on a plate.
2. **Sauté Vegetables:** Add a bit more butter or oil if the pot is dry. Add the
   **onions and mushrooms**, cooking for 3–5 minutes until softened and
   translucent. Stir in the **garlic** and dried herbs, cooking for another 30
   seconds until fragrant.
3. **Deglaze:** Pour in the **broth** and vigorously scrape the bottom of the
   pot with a wooden spoon to release any browned bits (this prevents the "Burn"
   error).
4. **Pressure Cook:** Place the seared pork chops back into the pot, stacking
   them if necessary. Secure the lid, set the valve to **Sealing**, and select *
   *Manual** or **Pressure Cook** on High.
   * **Cook Time:** 7–10 minutes for fresh chops (12–15 minutes for frozen or
   very thick cuts).
5. **Natural Release:** Once the timer beeps, allow the pressure to release
   naturally for **10–15 minutes** before turning the valve to Venting. This
   keeps the meat tender.
6. **Thicken the Gravy:** Remove the pork chops and keep warm. Switch the pot
   back to **Sauté**. Stir in your **cornstarch slurry** (or heavy cream/sour
   cream) and simmer for 2–3 minutes until the gravy thickens to your desired
   consistency. Taste and adjust seasoning.
7. **Serve:** Spoon the mushroom and onion gravy generously over the pork chops.
