## Convection Oven Adjustments  

## Standard options  

- **Reduce temperature by 25°F** (e.g., 375°F → 350°F) and keep the original
  time. Best for baked goods (cakes, breads, cookies) so the interior cooks
  through before the exterior over-brows.
- **Reduce cooking time by 25%** and keep the same temperature. Best for roasts
  and longer-cooking items (e.g., 60 min → 45 min).
- **Reduce both** — drop the temperature by 15 to 20°F *and* the time by 
  10% to 15%. A balanced middle ground for casseroles, potatoes, and mixed 
  dishes.

## Key tips  

- Check for doneness **5 to 10 minutes early** and use a thermometer for meats.
- Preheat the oven and leave space between pans for airflow.
- Some convection ovens have an **auto-adjust feature** that lowers the set
  temperature by 25°F automatically. Check your manual before making manual
  adjustments.
- Delicate items like quick breads and custards may need a smaller adjustment
  (10 to 15°F lower) to avoid uneven rising or over-browning.
