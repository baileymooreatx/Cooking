# Elderberry Tea  

Making elderberry tea in an Instant Pot is actually a great way to extract 
more flavor and nutrients quickly. Here’s a simple, reliable method:

## Ingredients

* 1–2 tablespoons **dried elderberries** (or ½ cup fresh)
* 3–4 cups water
* Optional:
  * Cinnamon stick
  * Fresh ginger slices
  * Cloves
  * Honey or lemon (added after brewing)

### Instant Pot Method

1. **Add everything (except sweeteners):**
   Put elderberries, water, and any spices into the Instant Pot.

2. **Pressure cook:**

   * Seal the lid
   * Set to **High Pressure for 7–10 minutes**

3. **Natural release:**
   Let the pressure release naturally for about 10–15 minutes. This helps deepen the infusion.

4. **Strain:**
   Carefully strain out the berries and spices using a fine mesh strainer.

5. **Finish:**
   Add honey or lemon to taste (after it cools slightly to preserve nutrients).

---

## Tips

* Elderberries should **always be cooked** because raw berries can cause stomach upset.
* For stronger tea, use more berries or reduce the water slightly.
* You can store leftover tea in the fridge for up to 3–4 days.
