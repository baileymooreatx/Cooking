# Instant Pot Chili with Beans (Dried, Soaked)  

Swap in pinto, black, or red beans as you like.

### Ingredients

| **Ingredient**      | **Amount**      | **Notes**                         |
|:--------------------|:----------------|:----------------------------------|
| Olive oil           | 1 tbsp          |                                   |
| Ground beef         | 1 lb            | 80/20 or 85/15                    |
| Onion               | 1 large         | diced                             |
| Garlic              | 3 cloves        | minced                            |
| Fresh Jalapenos     | 3 peppers       | seeded and diced                  |
| Dried kidney beans  | 1 lb            | soaked 8–12 hrs, drained & rinsed |
| Crushed tomatoes    | 1 (14.5 oz) can |                                   |
| Tomato sauce        | 1 (8 oz) can    |                                   |
| Beef broth          | 1 ½ cups        |                                   |
| Chili powder        | 3 tbsp          |                                   |
| Ground cumin        | 1 tbsp          |                                   |
| Smoked paprika      | 1 tsp           |                                   |
| Cacao powder        | 1 tsp           | optional                          |
| Ground black pepper | ¾ tsp           |                                   |
| Salt                | 1 tsp           | to taste                          |

### Instructions

1. **Soak the beans** (the night before or at least 8 hours ahead): Cover with 
   2 inches of cold water in a bowl. Soak at room temperature for 8 to 12 hours. 
   Drain and rinse.
2. Set the Instant Pot to **Sauté**. Add the oil, then the beef; cook until 
   browned, breaking it up with a spatula after letting it brown on each 
   side for a few minutes.
3. Add the onion and cook ~3 minutes until softened, then stir in the garlic for
   30 seconds.
4. Add the drained soaked beans, crushed tomatoes, tomato sauce, beef broth,
   chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, cocoa powder, sugar, salt, and pepper.
   Stir and scrape the bottom to avoid a burn.
5. Lock the lid, set the valve to **Sealing**, and cook on **High Pressure** for
   **25 minutes**.
6. Let the pressure release **naturally for 15 minutes**, then manually release
   any remaining pressure.
7. Stir, check the beans for tenderness (they should be soft but not mushy). If
   still firm, cook another 5 to 10 minutes at high pressure. Adjust seasoning
   and serve.

**Toppings:** shredded cheese, sour cream, and chopped green onions.
