# Instant Pot Chickpea Curry

## Prep (the night before)

1. Sort and rinse 1 cup dried chickpeas (~2 cups cooked).
2. Soak in plenty of cold water for **8 to 12 hours** (or overnight).
3. Drain and rinse before cooking.

## Ingredients

| **Ingredient**                      | **Amount**                                 |
|:------------------------------------|:-------------------------------------------|
| Dried chickpeas, soaked & drained   | 1 cup (≈ 2 cups cooked)                    |
| Red bell pepper, diced              | 1 medium                                   |
| Full-fat coconut milk               | 1 can (13.5 oz) or powder (see below)      |
| Diced tomatoes (canned, with juice) | 1 can (14.5 oz)                            |
| Onion, diced                        | 1 medium                                   |
| Garlic, minced                      | 3 cloves                                   |
| Fresh ginger, grated                | 1 tbsp                                     |
| Curry powder                        | 1 tbsp                                     |
| Garam masala                        | 1 tsp                                      |
| Turmeric                            | ½ tsp                                      |
| Tomato paste                        | 1 tbsp                                     |
| Vegetable broth or water            | ½ cup (to deglaze) and 1 cup (for cooking) |
| Coconut oil or neutral oil          | 1 tbsp                                     |
| Fresh spinach (optional)            | 2 cups, chopped                            |
| Cilantro & lime (for serving)       | to taste                                   |

## Instructions

1. **Sauté aromatics.** Set to **Sauté (Normal)**. Add oil, then the onion and
   **diced red bell pepper**. Cook until softened, ~5 min. Add garlic and
   ginger; cook 30 more seconds.

2. **Toast the spices.** Stir in curry powder, garam masala, and turmeric.  
   Cook ~30 seconds until fragrant.

3. **Deglaze.** Pour in the ½ cup broth/water and scrape the bottom to release
   any stuck bits.

4. **Add everything.** Stir in the tomatoes (with juice), coconut milk, tomato
   paste, soaked chickpeas, and the extra **1 cup broth/water**. If using
   spinach, add it now.

5. **Pressure cook.** Lock the lid, set to **Sealing**, and select **Pressure
   Cook / Manual → High** for **15 minutes**.

6. **Release & finish.** Let the pressure release naturally for **10 minutes**,
   then quick-release any remaining pressure. Stir, taste, and adjust salt. If
   the chickpeas need a few more minutes, pressure cook an additional 5 minutes.  

## To Use Coconut Milk Powder

Replace the **1 can (13.5 oz) full-fat coconut milk** with:

> **½ cup coconut milk powder** whisked into **1⅔ cups hot water**

This gives you roughly the same volume and richness as a standard 13.5 oz can.

### Tips

- **Whisk into hot (not boiling) water** to avoid lumps. Start with a little
  less water (about 1¼ cups) and thin to taste — a paste is easy to loosen, a
  watery mix isn't.
- **Ratios vary by brand** because fat content differs. Check your package; if
  it says "1:3 powder-to-water for standard milk," you can go with ⅓ cup
  powder + 1 cup water for a lighter result.
- The curry will be **slightly less creamy** than with canned full-fat coconut
  milk. If you want extra body, bump the powder to **⅔ cup** and keep the water
  at 1⅔ cups.

## Serving

Serve over quinoa.  
Top with fresh cilantro and a squeeze of lime.
