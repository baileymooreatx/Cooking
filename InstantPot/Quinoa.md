# Quinoa

To cook **fluffy quinoa** in an Instant Pot, follow this reliable method.

### Ingredients & Ratio

* **Quinoa**: 1 cup (white, red, or tricolor)
* **Liquid**: 1¼ to 1½ cups water or broth
    * *Note*: A **1:1.25 ratio** yields firmer grains, while **1:1.5** creates a
      softer texture.
* **Salt**: ¼ teaspoon (optional)
* **Oil**: 1 teaspoon (optional, prevents foaming)

### Instructions

1. **Rinse**: Thoroughly rinse the quinoa in a fine-mesh strainer under cold
   water to remove the natural bitter coating (saponin).
2. **Combine**: Add the rinsed quinoa, liquid, salt, and oil to the Instant Pot
   inner pot. Stir gently to ensure no grains are stuck to the bottom.
3. **Cook**: Secure the lid and set the valve to **Sealing**. Select **Manual**
   or **Pressure Cook** on **High Pressure**.
    * **White Quinoa**: Set timer for **1 minute**.
    * **Red/Black/Tricolor Quinoa**: Set timer for **2–3 minutes**.
    * *Note: The pot will take about 5 to 10 minutes to build pressure 
      before the timer starts.*
4. **Release**: Once the cooking cycle finishes, allow a **Natural Pressure
   Release (NPR)** for **10 minutes**. After 10 minutes, carefully switch the
   valve to **Venting** to release any remaining steam.
5. **Fluff**: Open the lid away from you and fluff the quinoa gently with a fork
   before serving.

This method typically takes about **15 to 20 minutes total** (including
pressurization and release) and consistently produces light, non-mushy results.
