# Instant Pot Pork Loin Roast with Vegetables

**Serves:** 4 to 6
**Total time:** ~1 hour

## Seasoning Rub

| **Spice**                    | **Amount**            |
|:-----------------------------|:----------------------|
| Kosher salt                  | 1 tsp (plus to taste) |
| Black pepper                 | ½ tsp                 |
| Dried thyme                  | 1 tsp                 |
| Dried rosemary               | 1 tsp                 |
| Garlic powder                | ½ tsp                 |
| Onion powder                 | ½ tsp                 |
| Smoked or sweet paprika      | ½ tsp                 |
| Pinch of cinnamon (optional) | ⅛ tsp                 |

## Ingredients

| **Ingredient**           | **Amount**                               |
|:-------------------------|:-----------------------------------------|
| Boneless pork loin roast | 2 to 3 lbs                               |
| Olive oil                | 2 tbsp                                   |
| Yellow onion             | 1, wedged                                |
| Garlic                   | 4 or 5 cloves, minced                    |
| Carrots                  | 3 or 4 medium, cut into 1-inch coins     |
| Sweet potatoes           | 2 medium, peeled & cut into 1-inch cubes |
| Mushrooms (cremini)      | 8 oz, sliced                             |
| Chicken broth            | 1 ½ cups                                 |
| Worcestershire sauce     | 2 Tbsp                                   |
| Bay leaf                 | 2                                        |

## Instructions

1. **Prep & season.**  
   Pat the pork dry. Rub all over with the salt, pepper, thyme, rosemary, garlic 
   powder, onion powder, paprika, brown sugar, and cinnamon. Let sit 10 minutes 
   while you chop the vegetables.  
2. **Sear the pork.**  
   Set the Instant Pot to **Sauté (High)**. Add olive oil.  
   When hot, sear the pork on all sides, about 3 or 4 minutes per side, until 
   nicely browned. Remove the pork and set aside.  
3. **Sauté aromatics.**  
   In the same pot, add the sliced onion and minced garlic.  
   Sauté 2 minutes until fragrant.  
   Scrape up any browned bits from the bottom with a wooden spoon to prevent a 
   burn warning.
4. **Build the pot.**  
   Add the carrots, sweet potato cubes, and mushrooms around the pot.  
   Pour in the chicken broth and Worcestershire sauce.  
   Add the bay leaves.
5. **Pressure cook.**  
   Place the pork on top of the vegetables (use a trivet if you have one to keep 
   it above the liquid). Lock the lid, set the valve to **Sealing**, and cook on 
   **High Pressure** for:  
   - **2 lb loin → 18 minutes**
   - **2.5 lb loin → 20 minutes**
   - **3 lb loin → 23 minutes**
6. **Natural release.**  
   Let the pressure release **naturally for 15 minutes**.  
   Then carefully turn to **Venting** to release any remaining pressure.  
7. **Rest & serve.**  
   Remove the pork and tent with foil. Let rest 5–10 minutes.  
   Slice against the grain. Spoon the vegetables and pan juices over the top,
   or make a quick gravy.

> **Quick gravy (optional):** Pour the pot liquid into a saucepan. Whisk 2
> tbsp ground flaxseed with 3 tbsp cold water and stir it in. Simmer 2–3
> minutes until thickened. Season with salt and pepper.

## Tips

- **Don't skip the natural release** — it's critical for lean pork loin. A quick
  release can make it dry.
- **Sweet potatoes** are denser than carrots, so cut them to the same 1-inch
  size so they finish at the same time.
- **Mushrooms** hold up well in the pressure cooker but will soften; if you
  prefer some bite, add them in the last 2 minutes of a second short pressure
  cycle.
- **Check doneness** with a meat thermometer: **145°F** for medium, 160°F for
  medium-well. The meat will continue to rise a few degrees during rest.
