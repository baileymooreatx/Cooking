# Cajun Red Beans

## Ingredients

* **Beans & Liquid:** 1 pound dried red kidney beans (rinsed and sorted), 4 cups
  low-sodium chicken or vegetable broth, 2 cups water.
* **Meat:** 12–16 oz andouille sausage (sliced) or 1 smoked ham hock.
* **Vegetables:** 
  * 1 large onion
  * 1 green bell pepper
  * 3 celery stalks 
    (all diced)
  * 4 cloves garlic (minced)
* **Seasonings:** 
  * 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  * 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  * ½ teaspoon dried basil
  * 2 bay leaves
  * 1 tablespoon Cajun seasoning (e.g., Tony Chachere’s)
  * ¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional for heat), 
  * salt and black pepper to taste.
* **Fat:** 2 tablespoons olive oil or bacon grease.

## Adjustments for Soaked Beans  

For **soaked red beans**, the pressure cooking time is a significantly reduced. 
While dry beans require 50+ minutes, soaked beans need only **10 to 15 minutes** 
on high pressure to achieve a creamy texture without becoming mushy.

* **Preparation**: Soak 1 pound of dried red kidney beans in water for **8–12
  hours** (or overnight). Drain and rinse thoroughly before cooking.
* **Cooking Time**: High Pressure **12 to 15 minutes**.
    * **12 minutes**: For beans that hold their shape well (good for salads or
      lighter stews).
    * **15 minutes**: For the traditional creamy Cajun texture where some beans
      break down naturally.
* **Liquid Ratio**: Use approximately **4 to 5 cups** of liquid (broth/water)
  for 1 pound of soaked beans, as they absorb less water during the pressure
  phase than dry beans.
* **Release Method**: A **Natural Release** of 15 to 20 minutes remains 
  critical to
  prevent the beans from splitting due to rapid pressure changes.

## Instructions

1. **Sauté Aromatics & Meat**: Follow the original recipe: Sauté the andouille
   sausage until browned and remove
2. Sauté the "holy trinity" (onion, celery, bell pepper) and garlic in the same 
   pot. Deglaze with a splash of broth.
3. **Combine Ingredients**: Add the **drained, soaked beans**, remaining
   broth/water, **green spices** (oregano, thyme, bay leaves, and basil), and
   the cooked sausage (or ham hock) back into the pot.
   **Do not add the other spices until after the beans are cooked.**
4. **Pressure Cook**: Secure the lid, set valve to **Sealing**, and cook on
   **High Pressure** for **12 to 15 minutes**.
5. **Natural Release**: Allow the pressure to release naturally for **15 to 20
   minutes**, then vent any remaining steam.  
6. Add the Cajun seasoning, cayenne pepper, and salt & pepper to taste.
7. **Thicken**: Remove bay leaves. If the liquid is too thin, use the **Sauté**
   function to simmer for 5 to 10 minutes or mash a portion of the beans to 
   thicken the gravy.
