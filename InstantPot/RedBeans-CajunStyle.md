# Cajun Red Beans (Soaked)

## Ingredients

* **Beans & Liquid:** 
  * 1 pound dried red kidney beans (rinsed and sorted)
  * 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth
* **Meat:** 12 to 16 oz andouille sausage (sliced) or 1 smoked ham hock.
* **Vegetables:** 
  * 1 large onion
  * 1 green bell pepper
  * 3 celery stalks 
    (all diced)
  * 4 cloves garlic (minced)
* **Seasonings:** 
  * 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  * 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  * ½ teaspoon dried basil
  * 2 bay leaves
  * 1 tablespoon Cajun seasoning (e.g., Slap Ya Mama)
  * ¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional for heat), 
  * salt and black pepper to taste.
* **Fat:** 2 tablespoons olive oil or bacon grease.

## Instructions

1. **Preparation**: Soak 1 pound of dried red kidney beans in water for **8 
   to 12 hours**. Drain and rinse thoroughly before cooking.
2. **Sauté Aromatics & Meat**: Sauté the meat until browned and remove.
3. Sauté the "holy trinity" (onion, celery, bell pepper) and garlic in the same 
   pot. Deglaze with a splash of broth. Bloom the **green spices** (oregano, 
   thyme, bay leaves, and basil) at the end.
4. **Combine Ingredients**: Add the **drained, soaked beans**, remaining
   broth/water and meat.
   **Do not add the other spices until after the beans are cooked.**
5. **Pressure Cook**: Secure the lid, set valve to **Sealing**, and cook on
   **High Pressure** for **12 to 15 minutes**.
6. **Natural Release**: Allow the pressure to release naturally for **15 to 20
   minutes**, then vent any remaining steam.  
7. Add the Cajun seasoning, cayenne pepper, and salt & pepper to taste.
8. **Thicken**: Remove bay leaves. If the liquid is too thin, use the **Sauté**
   function to simmer for 5 to 10 minutes or mash a portion of the beans to 
   thicken the gravy.
