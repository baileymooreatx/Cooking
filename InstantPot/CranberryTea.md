# Instant Pot Fresh Cranberry Tea

**Yield:** ~4 cups (4 servings)

## Ingredients

| **Ingredient**       | **Amount**                | **Notes** |
|:---------------------|:--------------------------|:----------|
| Fresh cranberries    | 2 cups (8 oz)             | Rinsed    |
| Water                | 4 cups                    |           |
| Cinnamon stick       | 1                         |           |
| Ginger               | 1 inch, peeled and sliced | Optional  |
| Cloves               | 4 whole                   | Optional  |
| Fresh lemon juice    | 2 tbsp                    |           |
| Honey or maple syrup | To taste                  |           |

## Instructions

1. **Add** the cranberries, water, cinnamon stick, ginger, and cloves to the
   Instant Pot. No need to crush the cranberries. Pressure will pop them open.
2. **Seal** the lid, valve to sealing. Set to **Manual / High Pressure for 5
   minutes**.
3. **Natural release** for 10 to 15 minutes (the pin drops on its own). Then
   vent any remaining pressure.
4. **Strain** through a fine-mesh sieve into a pitcher, pressing the berries
   with a spoon to extract the juice. Discard the pulp.
5. **Stir in** lemon juice and sweetener. Taste and adjust.

Serve hot, or dilute the concentrate with hot water to make a lighter batch. The
strained liquid keeps in the fridge for up to a week.

## Notes

- **Why only 5 minutes?** The cranberry juice recipe that's closest to this one 
  calls for 8 minutes, but for tea you want a lighter extraction — 5 minutes
  gives you flavor without going fully into "juice" territory.
- **Frozen cranberries** work identically; no need to thaw.
- **Tea base option:** Drop 2 rooibos or black tea bags into the strained liquid
  and let them steep 3 to 5 minutes off heat for a layered drink.
- **Batch size:** Double all ingredients (including water) for a bigger batch;
  the cook time stays the same.

## Dried, Unsweetened Cranberries

Here's how the recipe shifts for dried, unsweetened cranberries.

|             | **Unsweetened dried**                                    |
|:------------|:---------------------------------------------------------|
| Amount      | ¾ cup                                                    |
| Cook time   | 5 min (same as fresh)                                    |
| Sweetener   | Add honey or maple syrup to taste                        |
| Tartness    | Strong so lean into the lemon juice                      |
| Rehydration | Pressing them through the sieve helps extract more juice |

### Tips for unsweetened specifically

- **Soak first (optional):** Toss the ¾ cup dried berries in ½ cup hot water for
  10 minutes before adding to the pot. They'll rehydrate more evenly and the tea
  will be more flavorful. Use the soaking water in place of part of the 4 cups.
- **Expect a deeper, more astringent flavor** than fresh. The drying process
  concentrates tannins. The cinnamon and ginger really help here.
- **Color** will still be a bit duller than fresh (drying degrades some
  anthocyanins), but noticeably more vibrant than the sweetened version.

Everything else in the recipe stays the same.
