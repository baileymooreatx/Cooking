# Instant Pot Steamed Broccoli

To steam broccoli in an Instant Pot, the most critical technique is setting the
cook time to **0 minutes** on **High or Low Pressure** and performing an
immediate **Quick Release**. This method uses the time it takes for the pot to
build pressure to cook the vegetable, ensuring a **tender-crisp texture**
without becoming mushy.

## Step-by-step instructions

### Prerequisites

* **Broccoli**: Fresh heads cut into uniform florets or frozen broccoli (no need
  to thaw).
* **Liquid**: ¾ to 1 cup of water or broth.
* **Equipment**: Instant Pot, steamer basket, or the metal trivet included with
  the device.
* **Seasonings**: Salt, pepper, garlic powder, lemon juice, or olive oil (
  optional).

### Instructions

1. **Add Liquid**: Pour ¾ to 1 cup of water into the inner pot of the Instant
   Pot.
2. **Position Broccoli**: Place the steamer basket or trivet inside. Add
   broccoli florets, ensuring they sit above the water line. If using the trivet
   without a basket, cut florets large enough so they do not fall through the
   gaps.
3. **Seal**: Secure the lid and set the steam release valve to the **Sealing**
   position.
4. **Cook**: Select **Pressure Cook** (or Manual). Set the pressure to **High** 
   (for softer broccoli) or **Low** (for crisp-tender). Set the timer to **0
   minutes**.
    * *Note for Frozen Broccoli:* Use the same 0-minute setting. If you prefer
      softer frozen broccoli, increase to 1 minute.
5. **Quick Release**: As soon as the Instant Pot beeps indicating the cycle is
   complete, immediately switch the valve to **Venting** to perform a Quick
   Release. Do not let the pressure release naturally, as this will overcook the
   broccoli.
6. **Serve**: Once the float valve drops, open the lid and remove the broccoli
   immediately. Season to taste while warm.
