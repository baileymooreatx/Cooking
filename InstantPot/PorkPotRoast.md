# Pork Pot Roast

Sear the pork, sauté the onions and mushrooms, then pressure cook on high, and
finish with a quick gravy. Here's the method for a ~2 lb pork shoulder (butt)
or pork loin roast.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb pork roast, salt & pepper
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cups sliced mushrooms (baby bella/cremini)
- 1 large yellow onion
- 2 tbsp minced garlic
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 cup chicken or beef broth  
- Cornstarch slurry (1 tbsp cornstarch and 2 tbsp water) for gravy

## Instructions  

1. **Sear the roast.** Set the Instant Pot to *Sauté* (high). Add oil, and when
   hot, sear the seasoned pork ~2 or 3 min per side until browned. Remove 
   and set aside.
2. **Cook the veggies.** Still on *Sauté*, add the onions and cook for ~4 
   min, add the mushrooms and cook for another ~4 min
   Stir in the garlic and bay leaves; cook until fragrant.
3. **Deglaze.** Pour in the broth and scrape up the browned bits from the bottom 
   to prevent a *Burn* error.
4. **Pressure cook.** Return the pork on top, lock the lid, set the valve to
   **Sealing**, and run **Manual/High Pressure** for 
   * ~55 to 60 min for shoulder (butt) roast 
     (about 25–30 min per pound for a 2 to 3 lb roast)
   * ~25 to 30 minutes for a leaner **pork loin** roast
5. **Release.** Let pressure release **naturally for 10 to 15 min**, then 
   vent any remaining pressure.
6. **Rest & slice.** Remove the roast, rest 5 min, then slice. Internal temp
   should be **145°F**.
7. **Gravy (optional).** Strain out the solids, turn on *Sauté*, and whisk in
   the cornstarch slurry until it thickens. Spoon over the sliced pork.

**Notes:** If you skip searing, it's a true dump-and-go recipe, but browning 
adds noticeably more flavor.
