# Lentils

Cooking lentils in an Instant Pot is a **hands-free method** that requires **no
soaking** and yields perfectly tender results in under 30 minutes. The general
rule is a **1:2 ratio** of lentils to liquid, with cook times varying from **0
to 10 minutes** depending on the lentil variety and desired texture.

## Ingredients  

* **Lentils:** Brown, green, black (beluga), or red lentils (rinsed and picked
  over).
* **Liquid:** Water or broth (vegetable, chicken, or beef).
* **Ratio:** **1 cup dry lentils to 2 cups liquid** (some methods suggest up to
  3 cups for green lentils).
* **Seasonings:** Salt, pepper, garlic, onions, or herbs (optional).

## Instructions

1. **Rinse and Combine:** Place rinsed lentils and liquid into the Instant Pot
   inner pot. Add seasonings if desired, but **delay adding salt** until after
   cooking if you prefer softer lentils.
2. **Seal the Pot:** Secure the lid and ensure the steam release valve is set to
   the **"Sealing"** position.
3. **Set Cook Time:** Select the **Pressure Cook** (or Manual) setting on **High
   Pressure**. Set the timer based on your lentil type:
    * Red Lentils: 0 to 4 minutes (0 minutes for soft, 4 for firm).
    * Brown or Green Lentils: 8 to 10 minutes (8 for *al dente*, 10 for
      soft).
    * Black (Beluga) Lentils: 6 to 8 minutes.
4. **Natural Release:** Once the cooking cycle finishes, allow the pressure to
   release naturally for **10 to 15 minutes** before manually venting any
   remaining steam. This prevents the lentils from splitting.
5. **Serve:** Open the lid, fluff the lentils with a fork, and adjust seasoning
   with salt and pepper to taste.

## Adding Meat and Vegetables

To add meat or vegetables to lentils in an Instant Pot, you must adjust your
technique based on the ingredient's required cooking time. The general rule is
to **brown meat first** and **sauté hard vegetables** before pressure cooking
everything together.

### Adding Meat

Meat generally requires browning for flavor and, in the case of beef, a longer
cook time than lentils.

* **Ground Meat (Beef, Turkey, Chicken):** Use the **Sauté** function to brown
  the meat with onions and garlic before adding lentils. Drain excess grease,
  then add the lentils and liquid. Pressure cook for **10 minutes** (the time
  required for the lentils), as the ground meat will finish cooking during this
  cycle.
* **Beef Chunks (Stew Meat):** Tough cuts like chuck roast need longer than
  lentils. **Sear the beef** on all sides using the Sauté function, then
  pressure cook the beef with broth for **10 to 15 minutes *before*** adding the
  lentils. Alternatively, cut the beef into very small cubes (smaller than the
  lentils) so they cook in the same **10 to 12 minute** cycle as the lentils.
* **Chicken:** Raw chicken breasts or thighs can be added directly with the
  lentils. They will cook perfectly in the **8 to 10 minutes** required for 
  brown or green lentils. Shred the chicken after cooking.

### Adding Vegetables

Vegetables are categorized by density, which dictates when they should be added.

* **Hard Vegetables (Carrots, Celery, Potatoes):** These hold up well under
  pressure. Chop them into small, uniform pieces and add them **raw** directly
  into the pot with the lentils and liquid before sealing. They will soften
  perfectly during the standard **10-minute** cook time.
* **Soft Vegetables (Zucchini, Spinach, Kale, Peas):** These will turn to mush
  if pressure cooked for 10 minutes. Add these **after** the pressure cooking
  cycle is complete. Stir them into the hot lentils and let them sit with the
  lid on (using residual heat) for 5 minutes, or use the Sauté function briefly
  to wilt them.

### Adjusted Instructions

1. **Sauté Aromatics & Meat:** Set Instant Pot to **Sauté**. Brown ground meat
   or sear beef chunks. Add onions, garlic, carrots, and celery; cook for 3 to 5
   minutes until slightly softened.
2. **Deglaze:** Pour in a splash of broth and scrape the bottom of the pot to
   remove browned bits (prevents the "Burn" error).
3. **Combine:** Add rinsed lentils, remaining liquid, and hard vegetables (if
   not sautéed).
4. **Pressure Cook:** Seal the lid. Cook on **High Pressure** for **10 minutes**
   (for brown/green lentils) or **4 minutes** (for red lentils).
5. **Finish:** Allow a **10-minute natural release**. Open the lid, stir in soft
   vegetables (spinach, peas), and season with salt.
