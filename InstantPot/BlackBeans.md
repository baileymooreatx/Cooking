# Black Beans

**No soaking required.**  
For soaked beans (8–12 hours), the cook time drops to just **10 minutes**.

## Ingredients

| **Amount**   | **Ingredient**                     |
|--------------|------------------------------------|
| 1 cup        | Dried black beans                  |
| 6 oz         | Pork belly, cut into ½-inch pieces |
| 1 Tablespoon | Coconut or Olive Oil               |
| 1            | Onion, diced                       |
| 4 cloves     | Garlic, minced                     |
| 3 cups       | Broth (chicken or vegetable)       |
| 2            | Bay leaves                         |
| 1 Tablespoon | Chili powder                       |
| 1 teaspoon   | Ground cumin                       |
|              | **Add after cooking**              |
| 1 teaspoon   | Smoked paprika                     |
| ¼ teaspoon   | Cayenne pepper                     |
| To taste     | Salt                               |

## Instructions

1. **Rinse** 1 cup dried black beans, picking out any pebbles or damaged beans.
2. Set the Instant Pot to **Sauté (Normal)**, add 1 Tablespoon oil, and brown the pork
   belly for **5 minutes** until crispy.
3. In the same pot, sauté **1 diced onion** and **4 minced garlic cloves** for ~3
   minutes. 
4. Add the dry spices at the end of the sauté step so they bloom in the fat.
5. Add the beans, 3 cups broth, and bay leaves.
6. **Seal** the lid, set the valve to **Sealing**, and cook on **High Pressure
   for 30 minutes** (25 min for firmer, 35 for softer).
7. **Natural release** for **20 minutes**, then vent any remaining pressure.

**Yield:** 1 cup dried beans → about 3 cups cooked. One pound (≈2 cups) yields
about 6 cups.

**Tip:** If the beans are still a little firm after the natural release, just
re-seal and cook another 5 minutes before venting.
