# Baby Bella Mushrooms

Cooking **baby bella mushrooms** (also known as cremini or portobello mushrooms)
in an Instant Pot typically takes just **2 to 6 minutes** under high pressure,
depending on the desired texture and recipe style. Most recipes recommend **5
minutes at high pressure** followed by a quick release for perfectly tender
mushrooms.

## Ingredients

- 16 to 24 oz baby bella mushrooms, cleaned and halved or left whole
- ¼ cup liquid (water or broth) to seal the pressure cooker
- 1 to 2 tablespoons of coconut oil or avocado oil
- Optional
    - onions
    - peppers (bell pepper or other mild peppers)
    - garlic
    - herbs
    - seasonings

## Instructions

1. Set the Instant Pot to **Sauté** mode. Add oil
2. Sauté onions, peppers, garlic, or herbs if desired
3. Add the mushrooms and stir for 3 to 5 minutes until slightly softened.
4. Pour in ¼ cup of liquid (broth or water) and stir well.
5. Secure the lid, set the valve to **Sealing**, and select **Manual/Pressure
   Cook** on **High** for **5 minutes**.
    - For firmer mushrooms: reduce time to **2 or 3 minutes**.
    - For very soft, stew-like mushrooms: increase to **6 to 8 minutes**.
6. Once cooking is complete, perform a **quick release** by carefully turning
   the valve to **Venting**.
7. Open the lid, stir, and optionally switch back to **Sauté** to reduce excess
   liquid or thicken the sauce.
