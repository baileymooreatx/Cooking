# Celestial Seasoning's Bengal Spice Tea

[Celestial Seasonings Bengal Spice](https://celestialseasonings.com/products/bengal-spice)
is a caffeine-free herbal blend.  

The full ingredient list is:

* cinnamon (38%)
* roasted chicory (17%)
* roasted carob, natural spice & vanilla flavors (11%)
* ginger (7%)
* cardamom, black pepper, cloves, and nutmeg (3%)

## Ingredients  

Here's a practical home approximation (yields ~20 servings):

| **Ingredient**               | **Amount** | **Notes**                      |
|:-----------------------------|:-----------|:-------------------------------|
| Ground cinnamon (cassia)     | 4 tbsp     | Dominant flavor                |
| Roasted chicory root, ground | 2 tbsp     | Gives the "tea-like" body      |
| Roasted carob, ground        | 1½ tbsp    | Adds sweetness/depth           |
| Ground ginger                | 1 tsp      |                                |
| Ground cardamom              | ½ tsp      |                                |
| Ground cloves                | ¼ tsp      |                                |
| Ground nutmeg                | ⅛ tsp      |                                |
| Ground black pepper          | ⅛ tsp      |                                |
| Vanilla extract              | ¼ tsp      | Adds the "natural flavor" note |

## Instructions  

Mix all **dry** ingredients thoroughly.  
Store in an airtight jar.  
Wait until brewing to add a couple of drops of vanilla extract.  

## Brewing  
Use **1 tsp per cup** of boiling water; steep **4–6 minutes**.  
Sweeten with honey or sugar and add milk if desired for a chai-style drink.  

> **Note:** The roasted chicory and carob are what give the blend its smooth,
> slightly bitter "tea" body without caffeine. They're the hardest to replicate
> at home. If unavailable, you can substitute a small amount of roasted dandelion
> root or simply increase the cinnamon slightly, though the flavor will be a bit
> sharper.
