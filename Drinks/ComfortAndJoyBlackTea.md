# Comfort and Joy Black Tea

[Comfort and Joy](https://www.republicoftea.com/products/comfort-and-joy-black-tea)
is a seasonal (fall/winter) black tea blend sold by
[The Republic of Tea](https://www.republicoftea.com/).

The official ingredient list for Comfort and Joy is:

* fine black tea
* cinnamon
* natural flavor
* cloves
* licorice root
* apple bits

Here's how to approximate it at home:

**Per cup (loose-leaf version):**

## Ingredients

**Per 18 oz mug (loose-leaf):**

| **Ingredient**                     | **Amount**                       |
|:-----------------------------------|:---------------------------------|
| Fine black tea (Assam or Ceylon)   | 1 Tablespoon                     |
| Ground cinnamon                    | ¾ teaspoon                       |
| Whole cloves (or ground)           | 5 crushed (or ⅜ teaspoon ground) |
| Licorice root, crushed or ground   | ⅜ teaspoon                       |
| [Dried apple bits](DriedApples.md) | ¾ teaspoon                       |
| Vanilla extract                    | a few drops                      |
| Water (rolling boil)               | 18 oz                            |

**Brewing:** Steep **3 to 5 minutes** in 18 oz of boiling water.  

## Instructions

**Brewing:** Heat fresh, filtered water to a **rolling boil**, pour **6 oz**
over the blend, and steep **3 to 5 minutes**. Add a splash of milk for a
"holiday spice cake" taste, per the brand's own suggestion.

## Batch  

For a larger batch, scale the spices proportionally and store the dry mix in an
airtight container. The "natural flavor" ingredient is proprietary, so a small
amount of vanilla or a pinch of nutmeg is the closest home substitute.

| **Ingredient**                   | **2 Week Batch** (14 18 oz mugs)   |
|:---------------------------------|:-----------------------------------|
| Fine black tea (Assam or Ceylon) | 14 tbsp                            |
| Ground cinnamon                  | 3½ tbsp                            |
| Whole cloves (or ground)         | 85 crushed (or 5¼ teaspoon ground) |
| Licorice root, crushed or ground | 5¼ teaspoon                        |
| Dried apple bits                 | 10½ teaspoon (3½ tbsp)             |

Wait until brewing to add a couple of drops of vanilla extract.
