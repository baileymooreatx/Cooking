# Steel-Cut Overnight Oats

The boiling-water bloom is great for texture and glycemic index
Makes 1 serving

### Ingredients

| **Ingredient**                    | **Amount** |
|:----------------------------------|:-----------|
| Steel-cut Oats                    | ¼ cup      |
| Boiling or Warm Water (bloom)     | ½ cup      |
| Kefir or Yogurt                   | ½ cup      |
| Salt                              | pinch      |
| Chia Seeds *(optional)*           | 1 teaspoon |
| Maple syrup or honey *(optional)* | 1 teaspoon |

### Instructions

**The night before:**

1. Place the oats and salt in a mason jar or lidded container.
2. Pour the boiling water over the oats, stir once, cover, and let sit **3 
   to 5 minutes** on the counter.
3. Cool to room temperature (about 15 to 20 min) before adding cultures.  
4. Add the kefir/yogurt and any sweetener. Stir to combine.
5. Cover and ferment at room temperature for **8 to 12 hours**.

**In the morning:**

6. Stir well. If it's too thick, thin with a splash of extra milk.
7. Top with fruit, nuts, nut butter, etc. Eat cold or microwave 30 seconds.

### Notes

- **Liquid ratio for the overnight soak:** aim for roughly **1:1 oats to total
  liquid** (water and milk combined) for a thick, pudding-like texture. Use 
  a bit more liquid if you prefer it looser.
- **Skip the drain step.** The small amount of bloom water stays in the jar and
  gets absorbed, so you don't lose starch or flavor.
- **Scaling up:** the bloom works the same at any batch size — just keep the 2:1
  water-to-oats ratio for the bloom step.
- The 5-minute bloom (vs. a longer simmer) plus the overnight cold soak keeps
  the glycemic impact lower than a fully cooked batch.
