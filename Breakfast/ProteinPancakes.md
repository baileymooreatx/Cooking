# Protein Pancakes  

**Yields:** 4–6 pancakes
**Prep:** 5 min
**Cook:** 10 min

### Ingredients

| **Ingredient**                                    | **Amount**       |
|:--------------------------------------------------|:-----------------|
| Plant protein powder (vanilla or chocolate)       | 2 scoops (~40 g) |
| Large eggs (or 2 flax eggs)                       | 2                |
| Plain Greek yogurt or silken tofu                 | ½ cup            |
| Baking powder                                     | 1 tsp            |
| Milk (oat or almond work well)                    | ¼ to ⅓ cup       |
| Apple cider vinegar or lemon juice *(optional)*   | 1 tsp            |
| Mashed banana *(optional, for moisture & flavor)* | ¼ cup            |
| Butter, oil, or cooking spray                     | for the pan      |

### Instructions

1. **Make a buttermilk:** Stir the vinegar into the milk; let sit 3–5 min.
2. **Whisk** eggs (or flax eggs), yogurt/tofu, baking powder, and the buttermilk
   until smooth.
3. **Add** the protein powder and whisk until no lumps remain.
4. **Rest** the batter 5 minutes — plant powders need longer to hydrate than
   whey.
5. **Heat** a non-stick skillet over **medium-low** heat and lightly grease.
6. **Spoon** ~¼ cup batter per pancake. Cook 3 to 4 min until bubbles form,
   flip, cook 2 to 3 min more.

### Differences From Whey Protein

- **More liquid** (¼ to ⅓ cup vs. 2 to 4 tbsp) — pea and other plant proteins 
  absorb far more moisture.
- **Longer rest** (5 min vs. 2 or 3) — plant proteins hydrate more slowly.
- **Lower heat** — prevents scorched edges before the center sets.
- **Optional banana + buttermilk trick** — masks the earthy taste and adds lift
  that plant powders lack.

### Flavors That Work Best

- **Chocolate** plant powder + banana + a drizzle of maple syrup
- **Vanilla** pea protein + berries + a spoonful of almond butter
- **Unflavored** pea protein + cinnamon + a splash of vanilla extract
