# Crêpes

[Basic crêpes](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/16383/basic-crepes/) 
Makes 8 — a simple, highly rated recipe from Allrecipes

**Ingredients**

- 1 cup milk
- ⅔ cup all-purpose flour
- 2 large eggs
- 2½ teaspoons vegetable oil (or melted butter for a more authentic French
  flavor), divided
- A pinch of salt

**Steps**

1. Combine the milk, flour, eggs, 1½ teaspoons of the oil, and salt in a
   blender (or whisk by hand) until smooth. Cover and refrigerate for 1 hour —
   resting the batter gives thinner, less lacy crêpes. For sweet crêpes, whisk 
   2 tablespoons white sugar and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract into the batter.
2. Heat a nonstick skillet or crêpe pan over medium-high heat and brush with the
   remaining oil.
3. Pour about ¼ cup (⅛ of the batter) into the pan and immediately tilt it in a
   circle to coat the bottom evenly. If the batter is too thick to spread easily 
   in the pan, whisk in 1 tablespoon milk at a time until it reaches the proper 
   consistency.
4. Cook until golden brown, 1 to 2 minutes, flip, and cook briefly on the other
   side.
5. Stack on a plate and repeat with the remaining batter.

Fill with **sweet** options (Nutella, fruit, powdered sugar, whipped cream) or 
**savory** ones (ham and cheese, eggs, mushrooms).
