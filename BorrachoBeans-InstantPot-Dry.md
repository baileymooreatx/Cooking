# 🍲 Instant Pot Borracho Beans (Dry Beans, No Soak)

## Ingredients (Serves 6–8)

* 1 lb (about 2 cups) dried pinto beans, rinsed
* 6 slices bacon, chopped
* 2 small onions, diced
* 4 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 jalapeño, diced (optional)
* 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
* 3 cups chicken broth (or water)
* 1 tsp cumin
* 1 tsp chili powder
* ½ tsp smoked paprika
* 1 tsp salt (adjust later)
* Black pepper to taste
* ½ cup chopped cilantro (added at end)
* Optional: ½ lb chorizo

---

## ⚙️ Instructions

### 1. Sauté the base

* Set Instant Pot to **Sauté (Normal)**
* Cook bacon until crispy
* (Optional) Add chorizo and cook through
* Add onion, jalapeño, and garlic → sauté 3–5 minutes until soft

---

### 2. Build flavor

* Stir in cumin, chili powder, paprika
* Add diced tomatoes and cook 1–2 minutes

---

### 3. Add beans + liquid

* Pour in broth  
* Stir well and scrape the bottom (important to avoid burn warning)  
* Add **dry beans (no soaking needed)**  

---

### 4. Pressure cook

* Seal lid, set to **High Pressure: 45 minutes**
* Natural release for **15–20 minutes**, then quick release remaining pressure

---

### 5. Finish

* Taste and adjust salt/pepper
* Mash a small portion of beans to thicken
* Stir in cilantro
* Optional: add a squeeze of lime

---

## ⏱️ Timing summary

* Prep + sauté: ~10 minutes
* Pressure cook: 45 minutes
* Natural release: 15–20 minutes
  👉 Total: about 1 hour 10 minutes

---

## 🔥 Tips for best results

* **Don’t add too much salt early**—beans soften better with moderate salt
* If beans are older, add **5–10 extra minutes** cook time
* For thicker beans: simmer on **Sauté** after cooking for a few minutes

---

## 🌮 Flavor upgrades

* Stir in a little chipotle in adobo for smokiness
* Toss in fresh diced tomatoes right before serving for contrast

---

## 🥄 Texture guide

* Want it brothy? Leave as is
* Want it refried-style? Mash more beans and simmer
