# Kefir Ranch Dressing

## Ingredients

* 1 cup plain kefir
* 1–2 tbsp olive oil (adds a bit of richness)
* 1 tsp dried dill
* 1 tsp dried parsley
* ½ tsp garlic powder
* ½ tsp onion powder
* ¼–½ tsp salt (to taste)
* ¼ tsp black pepper
* 1–2 tsp lemon juice or apple cider vinegar

# Instructions

1. Whisk all ingredients together until smooth.
2. Taste and adjust seasoning or acidity.
3. Chill for 20–30 minutes to let flavors blend.

## What to Expect

* **Texture:** Thinner than traditional ranch (great for salads; for dipping, use less kefir or strain it slightly).
* **Flavor:** Tangier and a bit more “fresh” tasting.
* **Richness:** Lighter—add a bit more olive oil if you want it creamier.

## Optional Upgrades

* Mix in a spoonful of Greek yogurt if you want it thicker but still lighter than classic ranch.
* Add fresh minced garlic or chives for a sharper flavor.
* A pinch of smoked paprika gives it a subtle depth.
