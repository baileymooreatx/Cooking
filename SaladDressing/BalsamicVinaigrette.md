# Balsamic Vinaigrette

## Ingredients  

* 3 tbsp balsamic vinegar
* 1 tsp  Dijon mustard
* 6 tbsp Extra virgin olive oil
* 1 small clove garlic, minced (or ½ tsp garlic powder)  
* 1 tsp  Oregano, basil, or Italian seasoning
* ¼ tsp  Salt
* ¼ tsp  Black pepper
* Optional: 1 tsp honey (or maple syrup)

## Instructions  

1. In a bowl (or jar), whisk together balsamic vinegar, Dijon mustard, honey, and garlic.
2. Slowly drizzle in olive oil while whisking continuously to emulsify.
   Alternately, just shake everything in a sealed jar.
3. Season with salt and pepper to taste.

## Ratio to Remember

A classic vinaigrette is 1 part acid : 3 parts oil—so you can easily scale or tweak it.

## Variations  

Creamier: Add a bit more Dijon mustard or a spoon of Greek yogurt  
Sharper:  Skip the honey for a more tangy dressing  

## Storage  

Keep in the fridge for up to a week. Let it sit at room temp for a few 
minutes and shake before using (olive oil can solidify when cold).
