# Elderberry Syrup (Instant Pot Method)  

Elderberry syrup is just a more concentrated, sweetened version of the tea, 
and the Instant Pot makes it really easy.  


## Ingredients  

* 1 cup **dried elderberries**
* 4 cups water
* 1–2 cinnamon sticks
* 1 tablespoon fresh ginger (sliced)
* 1 teaspoon whole cloves
* 1 cup honey (adjust to taste)

## Steps  

1. **Load the Instant Pot:**  
   Add elderberries, water, and spices (everything *except* honey).  

2. **Pressure cook:**  

   * Seal lid  
   * Set to **High Pressure for 15 minutes**  

3. **Natural release:**  
   Let pressure release naturally (about 15–20 minutes).  

4. **Mash & strain:**  

   * Lightly mash the berries to release more juice  
   * Strain through a fine mesh strainer or cheesecloth into a bowl  

5. **Reduce (optional but recommended):**  
   Turn on **Sauté (low)** and simmer until liquid reduces by about ⅓ for a thicker syrup.  

6. **Cool slightly, then add honey:**  
   Stir in honey once it’s warm—not hot—to preserve its benefits.  

---

## Storage  

* Store in a sealed jar in the fridge for up to **2–3 weeks**  
* For longer storage, freeze in small portions  

---

## Typical Use  

* Adults: 1 tablespoon daily  
* Kids: 1–2 teaspoons daily  

--

### ⚠️ Important Notes

* Always cook elderberries (never raw)
* Avoid giving to infants under 1 year (because of honey)
* If you have autoimmune conditions or are pregnant, check with a healthcare provider first
