## The Works

"The Works" is the house dressing at The Great Outdoors Sub Shop in Dallas — it
comes on every sub (and salad) by default and consists of:

* Lettuce
* Tomato
* Red onions
* Sliced tomatoes
* Wine vinegar and oil
* Oregano
* Parmesan
* Salt
