# Chickpea Flour

Chickpea flour is just finely ground dried chickpeas. It's one of the easiest
flours to make at home.

**Quick method (dry grind):**

1. Add 1 to 2 cups of dried chickpeas to a high-speed blender or a food 
   processor.
2. Process on high for about **1 or 2 minutes** until you get a fine, pale 
   yellow powder.
3. **Sift** through a fine-mesh sieve. Return any larger "grits" to the blender
   and grind again.
4. Store in an airtight container in the **fridge or freezer** for up to 3
   months (the natural oils can turn rancid at room temperature).

**A few notes:**

- A regular food processor works but takes longer and may leave a coarser
  texture. A coffee/spice grinder works in small batches (~¼ cup at a time) and
  gets very fine.
- If the flour tastes a bit raw or "beany," you can **toast it** in a dry
  skillet over medium-low heat for 5–8 minutes, stirring constantly, until it
  turns slightly golden and smells nutty. This is optional but makes a
  noticeable difference in baked goods.
- For your falafel recipe specifically, the ¼ cup of chickpea flour is a small
  amount — just grind up about ½ cup of dried chickpeas and you'll have plenty
  for several recipes.
