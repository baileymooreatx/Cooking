# [Lentil bread](https://youtu.be/Ws6rcyeCFMo?t=382)  
  
## Ingredients  
  
* 200 grams (1 cup) of lentils soaked overnight  
* 100 ml water (about 2/5 cup)  
* A pinch of salt  
* ½ tsp baking soda  
* ½ tsp vinegar  
* 4 Tbsp (¼ cup) of flaxseeds ground into meal
* 2 Tbsp of psyllium husk 
  
## Instructions  
  
Drain the lentils.  
Blend the lentils food processor with 100 ml of water.    
Transfer the paste into a mixing bowl.  
Add a pinch of salt.  
Activate 1/2 teaspoon of baking soda with vinegar and mix into the dough.  
Add 4 tablespoons of flaxseed ground into meal and 2 tablespoons of psyllium.  
Mix thoroughly.  
Cover the dough with a towel and let it rest for 15 minutes.
Shape the dough into a loaf with wet hands.  
Slice the loaf once lengthwise.
Bake on parchment paper for 45 minutes at 180°C/360°F.
Let the bread cool before slicing.
