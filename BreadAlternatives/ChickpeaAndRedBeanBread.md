# [Chickpea and Red Bean Bread](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ws6rcyeCFMo)  

## Ingredients  

* 200 grams (1.1 cups) of red beans soaked overnight
* 100 grams (½ cup) of chickpeas soaked overnight
* A pinch of salt
* 80 ml of cold water
* ½ tsp baking soda
* ½ tsp vinegar
* 2 Tbsp of psyllium husk
* 80 grams (almost ½ cup or ~7.8 tbsp) of whole flaxseed ground into meal  
  

## Instructions  
  
1. Drain the red beans and chickpeas.
2. Blend the red beans with 50 ml of cold water in a food processor.  
3. Blend the chickpeas with 30 ml of cold water in a food processor.  
4. Mix the ground red beans and chickpeas thoroughly in a mixing bowl.  
5. Add a pinch of salt.  
6. Neutralize half a teaspoon of baking soda with vinegar over the dough.  
7. Add 2 tablespoons of psyllium and 80 grams of flaxseed ground into meal. 
8. Mix thoroughly, finishing with your hands.  
9. Cover with a towel and let the dough rest for 20 minutes.  
10. Shape the dough into a loaf with wet hands.  
11. Slice the loaf once lengthwise.  
12. Bake on parchment paper for 1 hour at 170°C/340℉.
13. Let the bread cool before slicing.
