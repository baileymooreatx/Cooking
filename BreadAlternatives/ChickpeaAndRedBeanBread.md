# [Chickpea and Red Bean Bread](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ws6rcyeCFMo)  

## Ingredients  

* 200 grams (1.1 cups) of red beans soaked overnight
* 100 grams (½ cup) of chickpeas soaked overnight
* A pinch of salt
* 80 ml of cold water
* ½ tsp baking soda
* ½ tsp vinegar
* 2 Tbsp of psyllium
* 80 grams (½ cup) of flaxseed ground into meal  
  

## Instructions  
  
Drain the red beans and chickpeas.
Blend the red beans with 50 ml of cold water in a food processor.  
Blend the chickpeas with 30 ml of cold water in a food processor.  
Mix the ground red beans and chickpeas thoroughly in a mixing bowl.  
Add a pinch of salt.  
Neutralize half a teaspoon of baking soda with vinegar over the dough.  
Add 2 tablespoons of psyllium and 80 grams of flaxseed ground into meal. 
Mix thoroughly, finishing with your hands. 
Cover with a towel and let the dough rest for 20 minutes.  
Shape the dough into a loaf with wet hands.  
Slice the loaf once lengthwise.  
Bake on parchment paper for 1 hour at 170°C/340℉.
Let the bread cool before slicing.
