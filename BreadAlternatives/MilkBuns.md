# Milk Buns  

## [First Variation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mp4nHjZycnA)  

### Ingredients  

* 3 eggs  
* 1½ cups heavy cream powder  
* 1 Tbsp baking powder  
  
### Instructions  
  
1. Mix ingredients in a mixing bowl.
2. Form wet dough into buns using water on hands to prevent sticking.
3. Bake in a convection oven or air fryer at 350°F for 6 minutes per side.

## [Second Variation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yr5SDwzLpCU)  

### Ingredients  

* 3 eggs
* 1½ cups milk powder (goat, butter, or regular full fat)
* 1 Tbsp aluminum-free baking powder

### Instructions  

1. Whisk eggs.  
2. In a separate bowl, mix dry ingredients.  
3. Gradually add dry mix to eggs (½ cup at a time) until thick, dough-like consistency.
4. Wet hands, form into buns, place on parchment-lined tray.
5. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 12 to 15 mins, until lightly golden.
