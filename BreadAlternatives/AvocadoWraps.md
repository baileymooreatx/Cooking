# Avocado Wrap  
  
## [3-Ingredient Avocado Wrap](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yr5SDwzLpCU&t=507s)  

### Ingredients  
1 avocado
1 egg
50g grated cheese (Parmesan, cheddar, Gruyere)

### Instructions  
  
1. Blend or mash all ingredients until smooth.  
2. Spread thinly on a parchment-lined baking sheet.  
3. Bake at 350°F (180°C) for ~20 mins (mine took 30 mins).  

## [Second Variation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SvN_lC4VxxE)

### Ingredients

* 1 avocado
* 1 tablespoon lemon juice
* Salt to taste
* 1 egg
* 1/2 carrot 30 grams grated
* 40 grams grated cheddar or mozzarella
* Olive oil for greasing the baking paper
* Sesame seeds for sprinkling

### Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Peel the avocado and mash it in a mixing bowl.
3. Mix in salt and lemon juice.
4. Add one raw egg, grated carrot, and grated cheese.
5. Mix well.
6. Divide the mixture into two parts and form a circle with a diameter of 15 cm.
7. Bake in 350°F for 20 to 25 minutes.
