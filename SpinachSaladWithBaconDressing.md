# Spinach Salad with Warm Bacon Dressing

Serves: 2 
Preparation time: 20 minutes

## Ingredients

### Salad

| **Amount** | **Ingredient**                             |
|------------|--------------------------------------------|
| 4 oz       | baby spinach, washed and dried             |
| 2          | hard-boiled eggs, wedged                   |
| 4 oz       | white button mushrooms, thinly sliced      |
| ¼ small    | red onion, thinly sliced                   |
| 4 slices   | thick-cut bacon, cooked crisp and crumbled |
| ¼ cup      | shredded Swiss cheese (optional)           |

### Warm Bacon Dressing

| **Amount**   | **Ingredient**                       |
|--------------|--------------------------------------|
| 2 Tablespoon | Bacon drippings                      |
| 2 cloves     | garlic, minced                       |
| 1 Tablespoon | Apple cider vinegar                  |
| 1 teaspoon   | Dijon mustard                        |
| 1 Tablespoon | Honey                                |
| To taste     | Salt and freshly ground black pepper |

## Instructions

1. **Cook the bacon.** In a skillet over medium heat, cook bacon until crisp,
   about 5 to 7 minutes. Remove with a slotted spoon and crumble. **Reserve 2
   tablespoons of bacon drippings** in the pan.
2. **Make the dressing.** In the same pan with the drippings, sauté the garlic 
   over medium heat until softened, about 2 minutes. Whisk in the
   vinegar, Dijon mustard, and honey. Heat until just simmering, then remove
   from heat. Season with salt and pepper.
3. **Assemble.** In a large bowl, toss the spinach with the mushrooms and red
   onion. Top with sliced eggs, crumbled bacon, and Swiss cheese.
4. **Serve.** Pour the warm dressing over the salad and toss gently. Serve
   immediately.

**Tip:** Many home cooks report better flavor using the bacon drippings in place
of any added oil, and recommend plating individual portions and pouring the
dressing over each to prevent wilting.
