# Pork Breakfast Sausage

Making **pork breakfast sausage** from scratch is a simple process that involves
mixing **ground pork** with a blend of **sage, thyme, salt, pepper**, and
optional sweeteners like **maple syrup** or **brown sugar**. The key to great
texture is keeping the meat **cold** and avoiding **overmixing**.

## Ingredients

* 1 ½ to 2 pounds ground pork (preferably 80/20 lean-to-fat ratio for
  juiciness)
* 1 ½ teaspoons kosher salt (adjust to taste)
* 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
* 2 teaspoons dried rubbed sage (or 1 tablespoon fresh, finely chopped)
* 1 teaspoon dried thyme (or 1 tablespoon fresh, finely chopped)
* 1 tablespoon brown sugar or maple syrup (optional, for sweetness)
* ¼ teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes (optional, for heat)
* ¼ teaspoon ground nutmeg or allspice (optional, for warmth)
* 1 tablespoon oil (for frying)

## Instructions

1. **Chill the meat**: Ensure your ground pork is very cold; if grinding your
   own, freeze pork chunks for 45 minutes before grinding twice through a medium
   die.
2. **Mix seasonings**: In a small bowl, combine the salt, pepper, sage, thyme,
   sugar, red pepper flakes, and nutmeg.
3. **Combine**: Place the cold pork in a large bowl, sprinkle the spice mixture
   evenly over the meat, and add the maple syrup or brown sugar if using.
4. **Blend gently**: Mix with your hands just until the spices are evenly
   distributed; **do not overmix**, or the sausage will become tough and
   rubbery.
5. **Rest (Optional)**: For the best flavor, cover and refrigerate the mixture
   for at least **2 hours** or overnight to let the herbs meld.
6. **Form patties**: Scoop about ¼ cup of the mixture and flatten it into 2½- to
   3-inch wide patties, about ½-inch thick.
7. **Cook**: Heat oil in a cast-iron skillet over medium heat. Fry the patties
   for **3–5 minutes per side** until browned and the internal temperature
   reaches **160°F (71°C)**.
