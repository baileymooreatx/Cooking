## Baked Falafel (Convection Oven)

Preheat the convection oven to 400°F and line a baking sheet with parchment
paper. Convection cooks falafel more evenly, so total bake time is about 
**25 to 30 minutes**, flipping halfway.  

**Tip:** Preheating the baking sheet in the oven before adding the falafel helps
the bottoms brown from the start.  

## Ingredients  

| **Amount**   | **Ingredient**                                         |
|--------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| 1½ cups      | Dried Chickpeas, soaked overnight and cooked           |
| ½ small      | Onion, roughly chopped                                 |
| 3 cloves     | Garlic                                                 |
| ¼ cup packed | Flat-Leaf Parsley                                      |
| ¼ cup packed | Cilantro                                               |
| 2 tbsp       | Olive Oil (plus more for brushing)                     |
| 2 tbsp       | Lemon Juice                                            |
| 1 tsp        | Ground Cumin                                           |
| ½ tsp        | Ground Coriander                                       |
| ¼ tsp        | Cayenne (optional)                                     |
| ½ tsp        | Baking Powder                                          |
| ¼ cup        | [Chickpea Flour](./BreadAlternatives/ChickpeaFlour.md) |
| To taste     | Salt and Black Pepper                                  |

## Instructions  

1. **Process:** Add chickpeas, onion, garlic, parsley, cilantro, olive oil,
   lemon juice, cumin, coriander, cayenne, and salt to a food processor. Pulse
   10 to 12 times until finely chopped but not puréed (aim for a crumbly,
   couscous-like texture).
2. **Bind:** Stir in baking powder and chickpea flour. The mixture should hold 
   together when squeezed; add a little more flour if crumbly.
3. **Shape:** Wet your hands, scoop ~2 tbsp of mixture, roll into balls, and
   flatten into ½-inch-thick patties. Arrange on the parchment-lined sheet.
4. **Oil:** Brush the tops generously with olive oil (key for crispness since
   you're not frying).
5. **Bake (convection):** Bake at **400°F for 15 minutes**, flip gently, then 
   bake **another 10 to 15 minutes** until golden brown and crisp on both sides.
