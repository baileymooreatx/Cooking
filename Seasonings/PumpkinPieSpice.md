# Pumpkin Pie Spice

Whisk together the spices and store in an **airtight jar**.   
A common recipe (used by The Pioneer Woman, Betty Crocker, and others) is:

| **Ingredient**   |    **Amount** |
|:-----------------|--------------:|
| Ground cinnamon  | 3 Tablespoons |
| Ground ginger    |   2 teaspoons |
| Ground nutmeg    |   2 teaspoons |
| Ground allspice  |  1½ teaspoons |
| Ground cloves    |  1½ teaspoons |

**Method:** Measure all spices into a small bowl and whisk until the color is
uniform (ground ginger tends to clump, so whisk thoroughly). Transfer to a small
airtight jar and store in a cool, dry place for to 6 to 12 months.

Scale the ratio (roughly **4:2:2:1.5:1.5**) up or down as needed. If you
only need 1 teaspoon for a single recipe, combine ¾ teaspoon cinnamon with a pinch
each of ginger, nutmeg, cloves, and allspice.
