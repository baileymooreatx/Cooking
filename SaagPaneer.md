# Saag Paneer  

**Saag paneer** is a classic Indian dish featuring **soft paneer cheese**
simmered in a **creamy, spiced spinach gravy**. The key to a great saag paneer
lies in **properly wilting and blending the greens** and **frying the paneer
until golden** before combining them.

## Ingredients

* **Paneer**: 250 to 500 grams, cut into 2cm cubes  
  Imperial untis: ½ lb to 1 lb, cut into ¾-inch cubes
  See [Paneer Recipe](./Paneer.md).
* **Spinach**: 450 to 800 grams (fresh or frozen); some recipes also use 
  mustard greens for traditional "saag"
  Imperial units: 1 lb to 1¾ lbs (fresh or frozen)
* **Aromatics**: 1 large onion, 3 to 5 garlic cloves, 1 or 2 inches ginger, 
  1 or 2 green chilies
* **Spices**: 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp chili powder (adjust to
  taste), 1 tsp garam masala, salt
* **Fats**: 2 to 5 tbsp ghee, butter, or oil
* **Optional**: 2 to 3 tbsp single cream or yogurt for richness, lemon juice for
  finishing
  
## Instant Pot Instructions

The process simplifies the traditional stovetop method by combining the sautéing
and cooking of the greens into one pot. You start by using the **Sauté**
function to brown your aromatics (onion, ginger, garlic, chilies) and spices in
ghee or oil. Once fragrant, add the fresh spinach (and mustard greens if using)
directly to the pot; they will wilt rapidly.

Add a small amount of water (about ½ cup), seal the lid, and pressure-cook on 
**High**. Cooking times vary slightly by recipe:

* **Fresh Spinach Only**: 1 or 2 minutes at high pressure.
* **Mixed Greens (Spinach + Mustard/Kale)**: 5 to 10 minutes at high pressure to
  ensure tougher greens soften completely.

Allow for a **Natural Pressure Release** (waiting 5 to 10 minutes before opening
the valve) to prevent hot splatters and keep the greens vibrant. Once opened,
use an **immersion blender** directly in the pot to purée the mixture to your
desired consistency. Finally, stir in the paneer cubes (fried or raw) and simmer
on **Sauté** mode for 2 to 3 minutes to heat the cheese and thicken the gravy.

### Key Tips for Instant Pot Success

* **Deglaze Well**: After sautéing onions and spices, scrape the bottom of the
  pot thoroughly with a little water before pressure-cooking to avoid the "Burn"
  warning.
* **Paneer Texture**: For the best texture, fry the paneer cubes separately in a
  pan while the greens are cooking, before adding them to the finished gravy in 
  the Instant Pot.  
  If adding raw paneer directly, soak the cubes in warm salted water beforehand 
  to ensure they remain soft.  
  **Fry the Paneer**: Toss paneer cubes with a pinch of turmeric, chili
  powder, and salt. Fry in hot oil or ghee over medium-high heat until golden 
  brown on all sides (about 5 to 8 minutes). Remove and set aside; some recipes 
  suggest soaking fried paneer in warm water to keep it soft.  
* **Color Retention**: To keep the saag bright green, some cooks add a few ice
  cubes before blending or immediately transfer the hot purée to a blender 
  (carefully) to stop the cooking process, though an immersion blender is safer
  and easier.
  
## Traditional instructions
  
1. **Prepare the Paneer**: Toss paneer cubes with a pinch of turmeric, chili
   powder, and salt. Fry in hot oil or ghee over medium-high heat until golden
   brown on all sides (about 5 to 8 minutes). Remove and set aside; some recipes
   suggest soaking fried paneer in warm water to keep it soft.
2. **Cook the Greens**: If using fresh spinach, blanch in boiling water for 2
   minutes, then chill in ice water and drain. If using frozen spinach, thaw and
   squeeze out excess water. Roughly chop or leave whole for blending later.
3. **Sauté Aromatics**: Heat ghee or oil in a pan. Add cumin seeds and let them
   sizzle. Add finely chopped (or blended) onion, garlic, ginger, and green
   chilies. Sauté until the onion turns golden brown or "toffee-colored" (about
   8 to 15 minutes).
4. **Add Spices**: Stir in turmeric, chili powder, and garam masala. Cook for
   1 or 2 minutes until fragrant, adding a splash of water if the spices stick.
5. **Blend the Base**: Add the cooked spinach to the pan with a little water.
   Simmer for 5 minutes, then blend the mixture using an immersion blender or
   food processor until smooth or slightly textured, according to preference.
6. **Simmer**: Return the green purée to the pan. Stir in yogurt or cream if
   using. Add the fried paneer cubes and simmer for 3–5 minutes to let flavors
   meld.
7. **Finish**: Season with salt and a squeeze of lemon juice. Serve hot with
   naan, roti, or rice.
