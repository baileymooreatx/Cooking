# 🥣 Kefir Blue Cheese Dressing  

Kefir works really well in blue cheese dressing. 
It keeps the bold flavor of the cheese but makes 
the dressing lighter and tangier.

## Ingredients  

* ¾ cup plain kefir
* ½ cup crumbled blue cheese
* 1–2 tbsp olive oil (optional, for richness)
* 1 tbsp lemon juice or white vinegar
* ¼ tsp garlic powder (or 1 small fresh clove, minced)
* ¼ tsp black pepper
* Pinch of salt (go light—cheese is already salty)

## Instructions  

1. In a bowl, mash about half of the blue cheese with a fork (this helps flavor the base).
2. Whisk in the kefir, olive oil, and lemon juice/vinegar.
3. Stir in the remaining blue cheese crumbles for texture.
4. Add garlic, pepper, and salt to taste.
5. Chill for at least 30 minutes before serving.

## Texture Tips  

For dip (thicker)
* Use less kefir, or
* Mix in a couple tablespoons of Greek yogurt, or
* Let it sit in the fridge longer. It thickens slightly.

## Flavor Variations

* Add a dash of Worcestershire sauce for depth.
* Stir in chopped chives or parsley for freshness.
* A tiny drizzle of honey balances the sharpness if your blue cheese is very strong.
